# Stripe onboarding — what kind of business this is

**Site:** https://smallplanevalue.com  
**Entity:** use the legal name that will receive the money (likely Back in the Saddle, LLC, if that’s the Stripe account).

Stripe will look at this form **and** the website. The site already says the product is **not a certified appraisal**. Describe the business the same way here so they don’t pause the account for a mismatch.

---

## What to select (industry / type)

**Pick software / SaaS / digital products** — not appraisal, not consulting.

| Option you might see | Use it? |
|----------------------|---------|
| Software, SaaS, computer software, digital goods, information services | **Yes** — this is it |
| Consulting / management consulting | No — you are not billing hourly advice |
| Appraisal / real estate appraisal / professional appraisal | No — you do not issue certified appraisals |
| Professional services (catch-all) | Only if there is no software/SaaS choice |

You sell **self-serve digital reports** on a website. Customers pay for a valuation (and checklist) in the product, not for a licensed appraiser’s opinion used in a loan or insurance file.

Calling it an “appraisal business” on Stripe while smallplanevalue.com says “Not a certified appraisal” is how accounts get held for review.

## Narrative for the description box (paste this)

```
SmallPlaneValue.com is an online software service for general aviation pilots. Customers pay for digital aircraft valuation reports and pre-buy checklists. A report estimates typical asking range, fair market value, and a buyer offer target for a specific used airplane (piston, twin, LSA, or experimental). Estimates are for research and shopping only — not certified appraisals and not for financing, insurance, or legal transactions. Payments are one-time (per report or a report pack) through the website.
```

Shorter if the box is tight:

```
Online software: customers buy digital used-aircraft valuation reports and pre-buy checklists at smallplanevalue.com. Research estimates only, not certified appraisals. One-time payments per report or report pack.
```

## Why not “consulting”

Consulting is project or hourly advice. SmallPlaneValue is a website: paste a listing or N-number, get a report. Stripe should classify that with **software**, same as other paid online tools.
