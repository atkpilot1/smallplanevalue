# SmallPlaneValue — Competitive Positioning

**Audience:** Founders, partners, social posts  
**Date:** July 2026

## One-liner

FindAircraft helps you find the plane. Windsock sells a valuation report. SmallPlaneValue is the free negotiation brief for the listing in front of you — plus pre-buy checklist — without pretending GA has MLS sold prices.

## Comparison

| | **FindAircraft** | **Windsock** | **SmallPlaneValue** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Job | Listings + market intel search (aggregate ads, sales history, alerts) | Instant valuation *report* product (AOPA partner, paid deep dives) | Free buyer workflow for *this* listing |
| Output | Comps, trends, accident/FAA context around listings | Account + Verified Value report | Ask / fair-market / buyer target + transparent negotiation bands |
| Honest gap | Strong on “what’s for sale / what sold” | Strong on polished report + distribution | Explicit: GA sold prices aren’t public — no fabricated sold comps |
| Audience | Brokers, serious shoppers, research accounts | Owners, insurance, AOPA members | Pilots shopping piston / twin / LSA / experimental right now |
| Bundle | Search + alerts | Valuation (+ logbook explorer in their stack) | N-number → valuation → pre-buy checklist → report a sale |

## How to talk about it (without trash-talking)

- **vs FindAircraft:** They excel at discovery and market research across thousands of listings. We start after you’ve already found a candidate — “what should I offer on *this* plane with *this* panel and engine time?”
- **vs Windsock:** They deliver a polished, account-based valuation report with strong institutional distribution (AOPA). We’re free in beta, show our work with ask vs fair-market bands, and wrap valuation with N-number lookup and a pre-buy checklist in one flow.
- **Our honesty edge:** Unlike real estate, GA sale prices aren’t publicly recorded. We show asking ranges and negotiation bands instead of inventing sold comps.
