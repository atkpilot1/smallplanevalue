# SmallPlaneValue — Competitive Positioning

**Audience:** Founders, partners, social posts  
**Date:** July 2026

## One-liner

FindAircraft helps you find the plane. VREF serves formal appraisal values. Windsock sells a paid valuation report. SmallPlaneValue is the negotiation brief for the listing in front of you — asking range, fair market, buyer target, plus pre-buy checklist — without pretending GA has MLS sold prices. A few free valuations to try; then pay-per-use.

## How to talk about it (without trash-talking)

- **vs FindAircraft:** They excel at discovery and market research across thousands of listings. We start after you’ve already found a candidate — “what should I offer on *this* plane with *this* panel and engine time?”
- **vs VREF:** They serve lenders, insurers, and formal appraisal needs with decades of transaction-grounded methodology. We’re for the pilot shopping a listing who needs a negotiation brief and checklist — not a bank-grade appraisal certificate.
- **vs Windsock:** They deliver a polished, account-based valuation report with strong institutional distribution (AOPA). We show our work with ask vs fair-market bands, wrap N-number lookup + pre-buy checklist in one flow, and charge pay-per-use after a short free try — not a subscription-first report product.
- **Our honesty edge:** Unlike real estate, GA sale prices aren’t publicly recorded. We show asking ranges and negotiation bands instead of inventing sold comps.
