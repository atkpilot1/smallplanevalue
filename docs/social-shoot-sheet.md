# SmallPlaneValue — Shoot sheet + Instagram clip

**One afternoon. One airplane. Three cuts.**  
YouTube long · Facebook/YouTube short · Instagram Reel.

Story: [social-ads-story.md](./social-ads-story.md)  
Site: https://smallplanevalue.com

You are not briefing an approach. You are pricing a listing.

---

## Before you press record

**Pick one airplane** you actually know (yours, a club plane, or a public listing). Write this on a notecard and keep it in frame with the laptop:

| Field | Example (swap yours) |
|--------|----------------------|
| N-number | N123AB |
| Type | 1979 Cessna 182Q |
| Ask | $289,000 |
| Hook line | “This listing is $289,000. What should you actually offer?” |

Run the valuation **once before the shoot** so you know the three numbers and aren’t surprised on camera. If the public listing sells, keep the video — say “this was listed at…” not “buy this plane.”

**Do not** film plates, briefings, or a takeoff as the hero. Ramp/hangar texture only.

---

## Kit (keep it small)

- Phone on a tripod (9:16 and 16:9 — see framing below)
- Second phone or laptop for screen recording (QuickTime / iOS screen record of smallplanevalue.com)
- Lav or AirPods for A-roll; hangar echo is fine, wind is not
- Printed listing or Controller/TAP page on the phone
- Logbooks in a stack (closed is fine)
- Navy/gold end card on your phone Notes app: **smallplanevalue.com**
- Extra battery, hangar door half-open for light

**Framing**

- YouTube long: **16:9**, chest-up at a table, laptop screen readable over your shoulder
- Facebook + YouTube Short + Instagram: **9:16**, tighter, big captions, URL at the end
- Leave the **bottom 20%** of 9:16 clear of faces/text (UI chrome)

Film 9:16 first. Crop 16:9 from a second take or a second camera. Don’t try to punch a vertical video into a horizontal YouTube A-roll.

## A-roll: YouTube 4–6 min (buyer walkthrough)

**Setup:** Table in the hangar or kitchen. Laptop open to smallplanevalue.com. Printed listing beside it. Talk to the lens like a buyer in the club, not a YouTuber intro.

**Run of show**

| Time | You say (tight) | Camera |
|------|-----------------|--------|
| 0:00–0:12 | Hold the listing. “This 182 is listed at $289,000. If I write a check this week, what’s the number?” | 16:9, listing readable |
| 0:12–0:25 | “I’m not briefing an approach. You’re shopping. The plane is already on your phone. The number isn’t.” | Same |
| 0:25–1:10 | “Houses have sold prices. Airplanes don’t. Forums are a coin flip. Paying the ask is how you overpay. Bluebooks weren’t built for this listing.” | Cut B-roll 1–4 |
| 1:10–2:10 | Live demo: paste listing or enter N-number. Narrate what you’re typing. Don’t apologize for the UI. | Screen record + OTS |
| 2:10–3:10 | Read **ask / fair / buyer target** out loud. “Glass, SMOH, damage, a tired annual — that’s what moves this.” | Punch in on numbers |
| 3:10–4:00 | Open the pre-buy list. “This isn’t to scare you. It’s how you talk money after the inspection.” | Checklist |
| 4:00–4:30 | “This is not an appraisal. It’s the hangar number before you spend $1,500 on a pre-buy.” | A-roll |
| 4:30–5:00 | “Try *your* listing at smallplanevalue.com. If the number feels high or low, tell us. That’s how this gets good.” | End card 3s |

**Title:** `How to know what to offer on a used airplane (without fake sold prices)`  
**First line of description:**  
`The listing is on your phone. The number isn’t. Free: https://smallplanevalue.com/?utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=hangar_number`

**Cards:** link at 1:10 (demo start) and end.  
**Thumbnail:** listing price + your face + text `What do you offer?` — no airplane-on-final.

**Edit notes:** Hard cut the first frame to the hook. Kill “hey guys.” Burn in captions. Keep the three numbers on screen long enough to read.

## Shoot order (about 90 minutes)

1. Set lights / hangar door (10 min)  
2. B-roll table (20 min)  
3. Screen recording of the live valuation + checklist (10 min, no talking)  
4. 9:16 A-roll: 22s Reel in 4–5 takes, then 45–60s Facebook in 3 takes (20 min)  
5. 16:9 A-roll: YouTube walkthrough, two full takes (20 min)  
6. End card still (1 min)

Edit order: **Instagram first** (you’ll post it same night), then Facebook cut, then YouTube long.

## Other suggestions (worth doing)

**Same story, different door**

1. **Seller cut (15s)** — “What’s *my* 182 worth before I list?” Same three numbers, different hook. Owners share more than buyers. Film it in the same session: one extra 9:16 take.
2. **ntailnum.com** — Put the same CTA on lookup results (“What’s this plane worth?”). Cheaper than ads: they already typed an N-number.
3. **BeechTalk / type clubs after the video** — Don’t lead with the Reel. Lead with “ran this A36 / 182, tell me if the band is drunk.” Link the YouTube chapter, not the ad. That’s where calibration (and trust) happens.
4. **Pre-buy shops / A&Ps** — One-pager: “Send the buyer to smallplanevalue.com before they book us.” They want educated clients; you want the checklist used. No approach content; hangar printer is enough.
5. **AirLogbooks cross** — “Clean logs move the number.” One collab Story/Reel. Valuation stays yours; logs stay theirs.

**Distribution, not more filming**

6. **Pin the YouTube walkthrough** and put the URL in the channel banner.  
7. **Facebook Page** gets the 45s; **groups** get the text post. Mixing those up is how you get removed.  
8. **Marketplace bios / TAP signature:** `What’s it worth? smallplanevalue.com` — boring, works.  
9. **Retarget** site visitors with the 22s clip (Meta). Don’t cold-boost a 5-minute video.  
10. **Ask for on-camera “too high / about right”** from two people who already used the tool. That becomes Reel 2. Permission in writing.

**Don’t bother yet**

- TikTok unless you already live there (same 22s file if you do)
- LinkedIn until you have a broker/CFI pack
- Stock footage of airliners or a G1000 approach
- Competitor call-outs in paid ads

**How you’ll know it worked**

- Instagram/Facebook: outbound clicks + `valuation_completed`, not plays  
- YouTube: click-through on the card at 1:10  
- Comments that say “ran my N-number” — reply with the feedback tab, not another aircraft video

If watch time is high and valuations are not, the cut still looks like flying content. Pull it back to the listing and the three numbers.
