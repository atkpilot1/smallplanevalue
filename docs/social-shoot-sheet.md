## YouTube — 4 to 6 minutes

Sit at a table with the listing and the site. Talk like you’re showing a friend how to use it.

| Time | Say | Show |
|------|-----|------|
| 0:00 | “This [model] is listed at $[ask]. Before I make an offer, I want a number.” | Listing |
| 0:20 | “Used airplanes don’t have public sale prices. Forums disagree. Paying the ask is easy. Knowing the market is the work.” | You |
| 1:00 | Paste the listing or enter the N-number on smallplanevalue.com. | Screen |
| 2:00 | Read ask, fair market, buyer target. What would change them (engine time, glass, damage, annual). | Results |
| 3:00 | Open the pre-buy checklist. “This is what you take to the inspection.” | Checklist |
| 4:00 | “Not an appraisal. A starting number so you don’t offer blind. Try yours at smallplanevalue.com — tell us if we’re high or low.” | You, then URL |

**Title:** `How to value a used airplane before you make an offer`

No long intro. Captions on. Hold the three numbers on screen long enough to read.

## Other ways to market the same thing

You do not need more flying videos. You need more people with a listing to hit the site.

1. **Seller version (15s)** — “What’s my plane worth before I list?” Same three numbers. Film one extra take while the camera is out. Owners forward this more than buyers.
2. **N-number lookup (ntailnum.com)** — After someone looks up a tail number, “What’s this aircraft worth?” They already typed the N-number.
3. **BeechTalk and type clubs** — Post the result for a known model and ask “too high / too low?” That is how you earn trust and improve the numbers. Link the long YouTube, not a paid ad.
4. **Pre-buy / A&P shops** — One sheet: send the buyer to smallplanevalue.com before the inspection.
5. **AirLogbooks** — Clean logs affect value; one shared post. Valuation stays on SmallPlaneValue.
6. **Signatures and bios** — `Aircraft value: smallplanevalue.com` on TAP, Controller, marketplace, email.
7. **Retarget** people who visited the site with the 22s clip. Don’t put ad budget on the 5-minute video until the short clip converts.
8. **Reel 2** — Two customers on camera: “about right” or “you were high.” Written permission.

Skip for now: TikTok (reuse the 22s if you already post there), LinkedIn, competitor attack ads, stock takeoff footage.

**Working:** clicks and completed valuations.  
**Not working:** lots of views, nobody running a number.
