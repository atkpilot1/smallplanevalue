# How to do YouTube and Instagram for SmallPlaneValue

You are a pilot who built a **free valuation tool** for used GA airplanes. That is the whole story. Every video is about **smallplanevalue.com**.

**Line you can say on camera:**  
“You found the listing. I’ll help you figure out what it’s worth.”

Related: [social-shoot-sheet.md](./social-shoot-sheet.md) (what to film) · [launch-checklist.md](./launch-checklist.md)

---

## The narrative

When a pilot shops a used airplane, the hard part is not finding the ad. Controller, Trade-A-Plane, and type clubs are full of ads. The hard part is the number.

Houses have sold prices. Airplanes do not. Forums argue. Brokers say “the market is the market.” Paying the ask is how people overpay. Walking away from a fair airplane is how people miss a good one.

SmallPlaneValue is the tool for that moment:

1. Paste the listing or type the N-number.
2. See typical **ask**, **fair market**, and **what to offer**.
3. Run a **pre-buy checklist** for that make and model.
4. Tell us if the number looks high or low — that’s how it gets better.

We do not invent sold comps. We do not claim this is a certified appraisal. We give a starting number so you don’t offer blind.

**Who it’s for:** someone with a listing open, about to call a seller or write an offer. Also an owner about to list (“what’s mine worth?”).

**Who you are on camera:** the guy who built it, showing one real airplane. Not a YouTuber. Not a broker. Not a flight lesson.

Say this once, then demo:

> I’m John. I built SmallPlaneValue because I got tired of guessing what a used airplane was worth. Sale prices in GA aren’t public, so we don’t fake them. Paste the listing. I’ll show you the numbers.

## YouTube — how you should do it

YouTube is where people who are *already shopping* search “what is a 182 worth” and watch a demo. One good video beats a channel full of flying clips.

### Set up once

- Channel name: **SmallPlaneValue**
- Handle: `@smallplanevalue` if it’s free
- Description:

```
Free valuations for used GA airplanes — piston, twin, LSA, experimental.
Paste a listing or N-number: https://smallplanevalue.com
Not a certified appraisal. We don’t invent sold prices.
```

- Banner / end screen: **smallplanevalue.com**
- Default upload: add the link as a card and in the first line of the description

### What to post

| Type | Length | Job |
|------|--------|-----|
| **Video 1 (do this first)** | 4–6 min, horizontal | Live demo of one listing |
| Shorts | 30–45s, vertical | Same hook as Instagram; feed the long video |

Do **not** start a “weekly flying vlog.” If you post flying, it should be 5 seconds of a tail number, then the laptop.

### Video 1 — say this

Sit at a table. Listing on the phone. Laptop on smallplanevalue.com. Look at the lens like you’re showing a friend.

**0:00**  
“This [year model] is listed at [ask]. Before I make an offer, I want a number.”

**0:20**  
“Used airplanes don’t have public sale prices. That’s not a conspiracy — that’s just GA. So I’m not going to show you a fake ‘sold for’ sticker. I’m going to paste this listing into SmallPlaneValue, the tool I built.”

**1:00**  
Do it live. Type the N-number or paste the ad. Narrate: year, time, avionics if they matter.

**2:00**  
Read the three numbers out loud: ask, fair market, buyer target.  
“Glass, engine time, damage history, a tired annual — that’s what moves this.”

**3:00**  
Open the checklist. “This is what you take to the pre-buy. Flagged items are how you talk money after the inspection.”

**4:00**  
“Not an appraisal. A starting number. Try *your* listing at smallplanevalue.com. If you know this airframe and we’re high or low, tell me. That’s how this gets good.”

**Title:** `How to value a used airplane before you make an offer`  
**Thumbnail:** the asking price in big type + `What’s it worth?` + your face  
**Description (first two lines):**

```
Paste the listing. Get a number.
https://smallplanevalue.com/?utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=spv_social
```

Turn on captions. Put a link card on the demo, not at the end only.

### Shorts

Cut the first 30 seconds of Video 1, or film a vertical take of the same hook. Last frame: **smallplanevalue.com**. First line of the Short must be the listing price, not “hey guys.”

### Cadence

- Week 1: Video 1 + one Short  
- After that: one Short a week, one long video when you have a *different* model (182, then a Bonanza, then an RV)  
- Reply to every comment that names an N-number or a type. Ask them to run it on the site.

Search-friendly titles: “what is a [model] worth,” “used [model] price,” “aircraft valuation.”

## Instagram — how you should do it

Instagram is interruption. People are not searching. You have **two seconds**. The Reel is the product ad. The grid can wait.

### Set up once

- Name: **SmallPlaneValue**
- Bio:

```
What’s this used airplane worth?
Paste the listing.
smallplanevalue.com
```

- Link: `https://smallplanevalue.com/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=spv_social`
- Profile photo: the site mark (navy / gold), not a sunset takeoff

### What to post

Reels only, until one of them actually sends people to the site. Then a carousel is fine.

**First Reel (22 seconds, vertical)**

Cover still: `What’s this plane worth?`

| Time | Show | Say |
|------|------|-----|
| 0–2s | Asking price on the listing | “You found the listing.” |
| 2–6s | You, phone in hand | “What’s it actually worth?” |
| 6–10s | The listing, no fake sold tag | “Sale prices aren’t public. We don’t fake them.” |
| 10–16s | Ask / fair / offer on the site | “Ask. Fair market. What to offer.” |
| 16–20s | Typing on the site | “Paste the listing.” |
| 20–22s | End card | “smallplanevalue.com” |

**Caption:**

```
Free GA aircraft valuation.

Paste a listing or N-number → ask / fair / buyer target + pre-buy checklist.

We don’t invent sold prices. If we’re high or low, tell us.

smallplanevalue.com

#generalaviation #aircraftforsale #cessna #bonanza #pilot
```

Same day: Story with the Reel, a poll “Shopping for a plane?”, and the link sticker.

That same 22-second file is your Facebook video and your YouTube Short. Film it once.

### Cadence

- Post the first Reel as soon as it’s cut  
- Then one Reel a week: same structure, **new airplane**  
- Stories 2–3 times a week: a screenshot of a valuation (blur the owner), “too high or too low?”  
- Don’t post pattern work, sunsets, or “today’s flight.” It trains Instagram to send you flying fans, not buyers.

### If you pay

Boost the 22s Reel for **traffic to the website**, not for views. US + Canada, interests: general aviation, private pilot, Cessna, Beech, EAA. Ten dollars a day for five days. If you get plays and no valuations, turn it off.

## One afternoon, both platforms

1. Pick **one** airplane you know (or one live listing). Run it on the site first so the numbers don’t surprise you.  
2. Film vertical: the 22s Reel (several takes).  
3. Film horizontal: the 4–6 min YouTube talk.  
4. Post Instagram that night. Upload YouTube the same night or next morning.  
5. Share the YouTube link on BeechTalk / type club: “Here’s the band I got on this [model] — too high or too low?”

Shot list and kit: [social-shoot-sheet.md](./social-shoot-sheet.md).

## What “working” looks like

People clicking through and **running a valuation**. Comments like “I ran my 182.” Feedback tab: too high / too low / about right.

Lots of views and no valuations means the video looks like aviation entertainment. Cut it back to the listing and the three numbers.
