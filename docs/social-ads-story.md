# Marketing SmallPlaneValue on social

**Site:** https://smallplanevalue.com  
**Goal:** Get pilots shopping a used airplane to run a free valuation.

Related: [competitive-positioning.md](./competitive-positioning.md) · [launch-checklist.md](./launch-checklist.md) · [social-shoot-sheet.md](./social-shoot-sheet.md)

---

## What we are selling

SmallPlaneValue estimates what a **general aviation airplane is worth** — typical asking range, fair market, and a buyer target — plus a pre-buy checklist.

It is for someone who already has a listing (Controller, Trade-A-Plane, Barnstormers, a type-club ad) and needs a number before they call the seller or write an offer.

GA sale prices are not public. We do not invent “sold” comps. We show asking ranges and a negotiation band, and we ask pilots to tell us if we are high or low.

**Line:** Know what it’s worth before you make an offer.

---

## Voice

- Pilot to pilot who is shopping a plane
- Specific: year, model, time, avionics — not “powered by AI”
- Honest about the data gap
- Ask for feedback; that is part of the product

---

## Message (same on every platform)

1. You found a listing.
2. You still don’t know what to offer.
3. Paste the listing or enter the N-number at smallplanevalue.com.
4. See ask / fair / buyer target. Use the checklist on the pre-buy.
5. Tell us if the number looks wrong.

End every post with **smallplanevalue.com** on screen and in the caption.

## What to show

A real listing, an N-number going into the site, the three value numbers, the checklist. Hangar, kitchen table, or FBO is fine. Logbooks on the table are fine.

Do not fake a sold price on screen. Do not spend the video flying the airplane. This is a product demo for a valuation tool.

smallplanevalue.com

## YouTube

**Short (30–45s):** same as the Instagram Reel. Hook in the first second. End card: smallplanevalue.com.

Titles:

- `What is this used airplane actually worth?`
- `Paste the listing. Get a number.`
- `GA aircraft valuation — free`

**Long (4–6 min):** sit down with one listing and run SmallPlaneValue live. Explain why GA has no public solds, read the three numbers, open the checklist, send people to the site. Pin the URL when the demo starts.

Title: `How to value a used airplane before you make an offer`  
Description first line: `Free valuation: https://smallplanevalue.com/?utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=spv_social`

## Facebook

**Page / ads:** same short video as Instagram. Longer caption:

```
Looking at a 182, Bonanza, RV, SR22 — photos are easy. Price isn’t.

General aviation doesn’t publish sale prices. SmallPlaneValue gives a typical ask, a fair-market estimate, and a buyer target for the listing in front of you, plus a pre-buy checklist.

Free: https://smallplanevalue.com/?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=spv_social

If you know the model and the number looks off, tell us.
```

Boost for traffic, not video views. Exclude flight-school interest if you only get student-pilot clicks.

**Groups:** text, not a hard sell.

```
Free tool if you’re shopping a piston / experimental: paste the listing at smallplanevalue.com and get ask / fair / buyer target plus a pre-buy list. No fake sold prices. Feedback welcome if you know the airframe.
```

Obey group rules. Post in clubs you already belong to.

Clicks to the site, completed valuations, feedback (too high / too low / about right). Watch time without valuations means the video is not doing its job.
