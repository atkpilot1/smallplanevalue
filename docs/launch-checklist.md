# SmallPlaneValue — Launch Checklist

**Goal:** Get free-beta feedback from pilots on BeechTalk, Facebook groups, EAA circles, and marketplace bios.  
**Site:** https://smallplanevalue.com  
**Positioning:** see [competitive-positioning.md](./competitive-positioning.md)  
**Instagram / YouTube / Facebook:** [social-ads-story.md](./social-ads-story.md) · [social-shoot-sheet.md](./social-shoot-sheet.md)

---

## Channels (do in order)

1. **BeechTalk** — Use the Feedback tab “Copy for BeechTalk / FB” button, or Post A below. Post in relevant type / for-sale threads; don’t spam.
2. **Facebook groups** — Bonanza, Cirrus, RV / experimental, type clubs. Same blurb + link. Prefer groups you’re already a member of.
3. **X / Facebook Page** — Use share buttons on the Feedback tab; pin a free-beta post on the Page.
4. **EAA / Oshkosh angle** — Emphasize experimental / RV / LSA coverage. If AirVenture timing still fits: AirLogbooks is at Hangar B · Booth 2096 — natural cross-mention.
5. **Trade-A-Plane / Controller forums / marketplace bios** — Short bio + `smallplanevalue.com` in signature or “about” where allowed.
6. **ntailnum.com funnel** — Keep CTA pointing at SPV with UTM (`utm_source=ntailnum`).
7. **Partner cross-post** — Ask AirLogbooks to mention SPV; SPV shows them on the valuation loader tip.

## Ready posts

### Post A — Free beta, ask for feedback

```
Free GA aircraft valuations at smallplanevalue.com — honest asking ranges and fair-market estimates, no fabricated sale prices. Paste a listing or enter your aircraft. Looking for feedback from pilots — especially BeechTalk and type-club folks. Try it and tell us what we got right or wrong.
```

### Post B — Differentiation (no trash-talk)

```
FindAircraft helps you find the plane. Windsock sells a valuation report. SmallPlaneValue is the free valuation for the listing in front of you — plus a pre-buy checklist — without pretending GA has MLS sold prices.

Try it: https://smallplanevalue.com
```

### Post C — Partner / logbooks (optional)

```
Quick tip while valuing a plane: digitized logbooks often make aircraft easier to sell and protect value. We partner with AirLogbooks (airlogbooks.com) for that side of the workflow. Valuation + checklist free at smallplanevalue.com.
```
